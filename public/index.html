    YOUR FIRST MINUTE · 221B FIELD NOTES

    A detective’s field guide

    1. Move around London with WASD, arrow keys, or the touch pad.
    2. Follow the clues: compare the letter, decode the cipher, then repair the telegraph.
    3. Know your case: with 2–3 players, everyone is a detective. With 4–8, one player is secretly the culprit.
    4. For 4–8 players: after two clues, detectives get one suspect vote. Everyone must choose the same person to catch the culprit. A split or wrong vote lets the culprit keep moving.
    5. Finish together: get everyone still in the chase to Blackthorn House and investigate the door. A culprit can strike or jam a station; teammates can rescue anyone who is down.
    6. Play three rounds: detectives score for evidence and solving the case together. The culprit scores for sabotage and escape. Highest total wins; ties share the win.
